Research

My work spans gravitational-wave astronomy, computational astrophysics, and NASA-funded instrumentation, with a unifying interest in the physics of extreme spacetimes.

Black-Hole Ringdown & Quasinormal ModesOngoing

When two black holes merge, the remnant radiates gravitational waves in a characteristic pattern called ringdown, a superposition of damped sinusoids known as quasinormal modes. I calculate predicted Kerr quasinormal-mode frequencies and damping times for GW150914, GW200129 and GW170814, using public GWOSC catalog parameters. I convert source-frame predictions to detector-frame values with catalog redshifts, and I keep model predictions clearly separate from observational measurements.

Motivation: if general relativity is correct, a Kerr black hole's ringdown spectrum depends only on its mass and spin. I am also studying what observations can and cannot constrain about Schwarzschild and Kerr geometries.

Python · qnm · Jupyter · GWOSC open data
LUNA-CAST: Lunar Dust Nowcasting and Forecasting ArchitectureOngoing

LUNA-CAST is a Phase I technology-development project with NASA L'SPACE and NASA Marshall Space Flight Center, selected for $10,000 in NASA seed funding. As Experimental/Instrumentation Scientist, I contribute to instrument selection and measurement planning for laboratory investigations of lunar-dust behavior. I connect scientific objectives with measurable quantities, instrument functions and expected outputs, and I build campaign logbooks and reporting tools to support documentation and coordination.

Instrumentation · Measurement planning · NASA Marshall Space Flight Center
Mission Concept Academy: Chief ScientistPast Work

As Chief Scientist for NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy, I led the science subteam for a proposed mission to explore caves beneath the Martian surface. I developed science goals, measurement requirements and instrument concepts, and worked with the Lead Systems Engineer on mission operations and payload interfaces. I also contributed to discussions of risks, schedules, costs and design trade-offs.

Mission design · Instrument concepts · NASA L'SPACE

Broader Interests

Beyond my current projects, I'm drawn to compact-object astrophysics and to what gravitational-wave observations can tell us about black-hole systems. I'm also interested in how computational modeling and observation work together as detectors improve.
