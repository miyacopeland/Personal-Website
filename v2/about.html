My Story

If you made it this far: my name is Miyauna, or Miya for short. It's a pleasure to make your acquaintance.

For a while, someone in my life kept asking why I wanted to study physics. Over time I understood that it wasn't curiosity. It was a test of whether I would talk myself out of it. His conclusion never changed: without a lifelong background in mathematics, and without a mind wired a particular way, I wasn't a serious candidate for this field. He said it gently, as a kindness, which somehow made it harder to hear.

These conversations came after I had been selected as Chief Scientist for NASA L'SPACE's Mission Concept Academy, where I led the science requirements for a proposed Mars subsurface exploration mission. They did not change his view. I didn't argue with him. I decided that the most honest answer to that kind of doubt is not a debate. It is a record.

Doubt from outside tells you very little about what is possible. Physics does not belong to one kind of mind or one kind of background.

So I keep building. I am completing an A.S. in Physics at Austin Community College and transferring to the University of Texas at Dallas for a B.S., with the intention of continuing into the Fast Track M.S. program. I am studying black-hole ringdown on my own. The question behind it is whether the ringdown of merging black holes is fully described by general relativity. For now, my project computes Kerr predictions for catalog events and keeps them clearly separate from the measurements. I am doing this because I decided to, not because I was born with a particular kind of brain.

I am a Black woman who moved to Texas alone at eighteen and started this path at a community college. I want to contribute to gravitational-wave science at the highest level, and I know how that sounds to some people. I am grateful for every program and every person willing to take it seriously alongside me.

Alongside research, I work as a Project Coordinator on healthcare projects at Parkhill, Smith & Cooper, tracking deadlines, coordinating teams and keeping the paperwork accurate.
