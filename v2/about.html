Curious about gravity, careful with data.

I'm a physics undergraduate working toward a research career in strong-field gravity, gravitational waves and instrumentation for lunar and planetary science.

My Story

Most of what I care about comes down to one habit: being honest about what a result shows. In my ringdown project, that means computing Kerr predictions and keeping them visibly separate from measurements. In LUNA-CAST, it means documenting the campaign as carefully as the science.

I also work as a project coordinator for healthcare projects at Parkhill, Smith & Cooper, tracking deadlines, coordinating teams and keeping the paperwork accurate.
