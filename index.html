About Me

I am a physics student and aspiring astrophysicist with research experience in black-hole spacetimes, gravitational-wave ringdown, and lunar-dust instrumentation. Through NASA L'SPACE, I contributed to LUNA-CAST, a winning Phase I technology-development proposal selected for $10,000 in NASA seed funding. I plan to transfer to the University of Texas at Dallas in Fall 2027 to complete a B.S. in Physics.

In astrophysics, I hope to investigate compact-object astrophysics, gravitational-wave astronomy, and the observational constraints on black-hole systems. My interests also include computational and observational astrophysics, and instrumentation and mission concepts for lunar and planetary environments.

Beyond Research

I work as a project coordinator on healthcare design and construction projects, supporting document control, consultant communication and schedule-sensitive administration.
